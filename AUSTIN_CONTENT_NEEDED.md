# Austin, Texas Solar Content Collection Checklist

This document tracks verified local content, project documentation, licensing verification, contractor approvals, and customer testimonials needed to enhance the Austin, Texas location page (`/locations/texas/austin`).

---

## 1. Verified Local Project Data & Media

- [ ] **Verified Project Locations**: List of specific cities or approved general neighborhoods (e.g., Circle C Ranch, Westlake, Tarrytown, Mueller, Zilker).
- [ ] **Neighborhood Descriptions**: Approved neighborhood context (e.g., HOA restrictions, tree shading profiles, roof pitches).
- [ ] **Completion Dates**: Exact month and year of completion for verified Austin-area projects.
- [ ] **Project Classification**: Tagging as Residential, Commercial, Nonprofit, or Multifamily.
- [ ] **System Sizes**: Verified system capacities in kW-DC and kW-AC.
- [ ] **Panel Counts & Models**: Specific panel quantities, wattage ratings, manufacturers (e.g., Qcells, REC, Aptos), and exact model numbers.
- [ ] **Inverter Types & Models**: Microinverters (e.g., Enphase IQ8 Series) or String Inverters with Optimizers (e.g., SolarEdge).
- [ ] **Battery Brands & Capacities**: Installed battery storage models (Tesla Powerwall 3, FranklinWH aPower 2, Enphase IQ Battery) and usable capacity in kWh.
- [ ] **Mounting Configuration**: Roof mount (shingle, standing-seam metal, tile), flat roof ballasted mount, solar carport, or ground mount.
- [ ] **Utility Provider**: Associated utility (Austin Energy, PEC, Oncor, Bluebonnet, Georgetown).
- [ ] **Retail Electric Provider (REP)**: Selected solar buyback plan provider for projects in Oncor territory.
- [ ] **High-Resolution Photography**: Original, unedited before-and-after photos of verified Austin installations.
- [ ] **Installation Team Media**: Action photography of certified Logic Solar crews working on Central Texas roofs.
- [ ] **Customer Publishing Permission**: Explicit written release to feature project photos and general locations.

---

## 2. Local Customer Testimonials & Reviews

- [ ] **Austin-Area Reviews**: Direct quotes from satisfied customers in Travis, Williamson, Hays, and Bastrop counties.
- [ ] **Customer Identification**: Written approval to display customer names, general neighborhood/city, and review dates.
- [ ] **Original Source Verification**: Direct links or screenshots verifying review authenticity (e.g., Google Business Profile, EnergySage).

---

## 3. Contractor Registration & Utility Compliance

- [ ] **Austin Energy Participating Solar Contractor**: Verification of official registration and active listing on Austin Energy's participating contractor roster.
- [ ] **City of Austin Vendor Registration**: Registered vendor ID with the City of Austin for municipal project permitting.
- [ ] **City Electrical Contractor Registration**: Registered Master Electrician license details on file with the City of Austin Development Services Department.
- [ ] **Austin Energy Rebate Portal Access**: Confirmed login credentials and administrative access for processing residential and commercial solar rebates.
- [ ] **Contractor Training Completion**: Certificates showing completion of required Austin Energy orientation and safety training modules.
- [ ] **Texas Electrical License**: Verified Texas Department of Licensing and Regulation (TDLR) Electrical Contractor license number and Master Electrician details.

---

## 4. Local Presence & Office Verification

- [ ] **Physical Austin Address**: Internal leadership verification of a dedicated, staffed office or warehouse location in Austin that complies with Google Business Profile rules and local licensing.
- [ ] **Monitored Austin Phone Number**: Direct local area code phone number assigned to and actively monitored by Austin project specialists.
- [ ] **Austin Team Profiles**: Staff bios and photos for local Austin project managers and field engineers.
- [ ] **Local Partnerships**: Official documentation for partnerships with local Central Texas suppliers, roofers, or environmental organizations.
- [ ] **Community Involvement**: Event sponsorships, local chamber of commerce memberships, or educational workshops hosted in Austin.

---

## 5. Service & Product Offerings

- [ ] **Third-Party Repair Scope**: Leadership clarification on whether Logic Solar will service systems installed by non-operating third-party installers in Austin.
- [ ] **EnergySage Recognition**: Verified authorization and badge usage guidelines for displaying EnergySage Elite or Recommended status.
- [ ] **Local Awards & Badges**: Documentation supporting any regional renewable energy awards or business recognitions.
